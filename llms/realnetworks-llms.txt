# RealNetworks

> RealNetworks is a Seattle-based technology company that builds AI-powered computer-vision and secure-communication products alongside its long-running consumer media brands. Its portfolio spans SAFR (facial recognition for security), Kontxt (AI messaging security for CPaaS/telco), GameHouse (casual gaming), and RealPlayer (media playback). Public developer/API surfaces are limited: product APIs (SAFR, Kontxt) are delivered through authenticated partner portals rather than an open developer portal, and the historical Real Developer Program (developer.real.com) is no longer live.

## Products
- [SAFR](https://safr.com/): AI-powered facial recognition for security professionals; delivered via the SAFR platform and partner/SDK integrations behind an authenticated portal.
- [SAFR Guard](https://safr.com/): Retail crime prevention and loss-prevention computer vision.
- [Kontxt](https://www.realnetworks.com/kontxt): AI messaging/communication security for CPaaS platforms, Telcos, and MNOs; integrates via API into carrier messaging and voice flows.
- [GameHouse](https://www.realnetworks.com/products-services/gamehouse): Cross-device casual gaming platform.
- [RealPlayer](https://www.real.com/): Media playback and download application.
